## Intent

**Problem:** With the online-demo flag set, `/ocutalon/` still shows the local sign-in page: a judge gets no explanation of the demo, has to find the published credentials elsewhere and is not warned that Visual Trace is shared (FR-14, FR-15, FR-59). REL-2 has no online-demo run.

**Approach:**
- `OcuTalon.Web.Public.Login` hands the page to a new subclass in the public package, `OcuTalon.Web.Public.Splash`, when the flag is set. The splash is server-rendered with its own generated inline stylesheet and one inline script (`OcuTalon.Web.Public.SplashAssets`, generated by `web/scripts/tokens.mjs` from `web/src/signin/splash.css` and `splash.js`), both pinned by hash in AD-13's Content-Security-Policy. It reads the published values through `OcuTalon.Data.Public.Settings`.
- The texts are kept as the public package's own parameters, each matched to a new `splash.*` catalog key.
- Story 16.1's flag-on unit `online-demo` gains the no-session path checks and runs the new browser specs, including the REL-2 online run in `web/e2e-online/`.
- With the flag off, the local page is rendered byte for byte as before.

## Boundaries & Constraints

**Always:**
- **The splash's texts** are exactly those of the AC and EXPERIENCE.md › The splash screen. Times use EXPERIENCE › Formats:
  - tab title "Try OcuTalon"; `lang="en"`; the full logo, 240px wide, alt "OcuTalon", served from Login's five sign-in images;
  - the `h1` "Try OcuTalon in two minutes", the subheading "Break the feed, and approve the fix from your phone." and item 3's description, word for word;
  - the "Sign in to the demo" card (`h2`):
    - labels "User name" and "Password" in an 88px column beside two `type="text"` inputs, prefilled with `demoUser` and `demoPassword`;
    - a copy button per input, named "Copy user name" and "Copy password";
    - the line "Your session is yours alone and lasts until the next reset. Signing in again from this browser picks it up.";
    - the warning inline notice "Other visitors can see messages in Visual Trace, so don't type personal information.", as the element just before the full-width submit button **Sign in and start** (`name="IRISLogin"`).
  - the "Before you start" card (`h2`) with its five lines, in this order:
    - "The demo resets every {interval}. Next reset: {time} ({countdown})." `{interval}` is the setting in hours: "1 hour", otherwise "{n} hours". `{time}` and `{countdown}` are page times computed by the script. The countdown is shown short and is also given spoken in visually hidden text, both as `web/src/app/chat/page-time.ts` writes them (`pageTime`, `countdownText`: "in 1 h 42 min" read as "in 1 hour 42 minutes"; "in 9 min"; "in 23 s"). It recounts every second, and once the reset has passed it rolls forward by the interval.
    - "Your chat, phone, mailbox and orders are yours alone."
    - "Have Telegram? You can scan a QR code inside to get the notices on your own phone."
    - "Each session can send {limit} messages an hour in the chat or Telegram." `{limit}` is `messageAllowance`.
    - "OcuTalon can't run shell commands, and every action it takes is recorded in the IRIS audit log."
  - the Visual Trace card:
    - its text "Read-only Visual Trace account, for opening Visual Trace in the portal yourself (trace links on the page are already signed in). See each decision with its cost, and every step." The part up to and including the first comma is the card's `h2`, and the rest is a paragraph, with "for" in lower case;
    - rows "User name" and "Password" holding `readonlyUser` and `readonlyPassword` in `mono`;
    - copy buttons "Copy Visual Trace user name" and "Copy Visual Trace password".
  - a `footer` (contentinfo), outside `main`, holding:
    - "OcuTalon is inspired by OpenClaw and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the OpenClaw Foundation.";
    - the link "Install it yourself (README)", with an `external-link` icon hidden from assistive technology and visually hidden " (opens in a new tab)". It points to `https://github.com/jbrandtmse/OcuTalon#readme` (`web/src/app/links.ts` `README_URL`), with `target="_blank"` and `rel="noopener noreferrer"`.
- **Copy:**
  - A press writes the exact value with `navigator.clipboard.writeText`: the input's current value, or the row's value.
  - Success shows "Copied" in that row for 2 seconds and announces it once through the page's one polite live region.
  - Failure (a rejected write, or no `navigator.clipboard`):
    - "Couldn't copy. Select the text and copy it yourself." appears as a field error (`circle-alert` icon) under that card's rows;
    - it is announced and the value is selected;
    - it stays until the next successful copy in that card.
  - The script holds no UX text except the countdown words of `page-time.ts`' `WHEN_TEXTS`, which row 2 pins against that module. It reads every other text it shows ("Copied", the failure text) from the server-rendered page.
- **Sign-in:**
  - **Form contract.** The form keeps Login's `%CSP.Login` contract: action `Error:FullURL`, `InsertHiddenFields(Error:URL)` right after the form tag, and the fields `IRISUsername`, `IRISPassword` and `IRISLogin`. Its URL-encoded POST carries no field but `IRISSessionToken`, `IRISUsername`, `IRISPassword` and `IRISLogin`.
  - **Wrong credentials** (any error code but 864):
    - "That user name and password didn't work." appears under the button as `#ocu-error` (`tabindex="-1"`, `autofocus`);
    - the user field keeps the typed name and the password field is empty;
    - both fields are marked `aria-invalid="true"` and `aria-describedby="ocu-error"`, as the local page does.
  - **No OcuTalon role** (the "norole" state): the no-role text appears in `#ocu-error` with no field marked, and the fields hold the published values.
  - With no message, nothing has `autofocus`.
  - `?signedOut=1` shows no notice on the splash.
  - There is no show/hide toggle.
- **Envelope:** the splash sends Login's headers with its own hashes: `default-src 'none'; script-src 'sha256-<splash script>'; style-src 'sha256-<splash style>'; img-src 'self'; form-action 'self'; frame-ancestors 'none'; base-uri 'none'`, and `Cache-Control: no-store`. It has exactly one inline `<style>` (SplashAssets `CSS`), exactly one inline `<script>` (SplashAssets `SCRIPT`), no `style=` attribute, no external script or stylesheet and no font file. Every `src`, `srcset` and `href` is one of Login's five `IMAGES`, or the README link.
- **Layout:**
  - D `splash-canvas` (white in light, `bg-dark` in dark, by `prefers-color-scheme` only), in a column at most `splash-max-width` (960px) wide.
  - The two cards sit side by side at 1280px width. At 1280×600, in light and dark, the whole **Sign in and start** button is inside the viewport without scrolling.
  - Each focus stop draws the 2px focus ring with the surface halo. An input's ring is drawn on its input box.
  - Text meets D › Colors' pairs.
- **Settings:** every value comes from `OcuTalon.Data.Public.Settings` (`Get`, `NextReset`) and is HTML-escaped where it is written:
  - an empty `NextReset()` or interval leaves out the reset line;
  - an empty `messageAllowance` leaves out the limit line;
  - an empty read-only user or password leaves out the Visual Trace card.
- **Test discipline (Story 16.1's):**
  - Every browser context and every curl jar of the new checks ends with the group-wide `/ocutalon/?IRISLogout=end`, whether it signed in or not.
  - Every page request of the unit carries a unique `probe=<n>`.
  - Each spec creates what it reads.

  - stacks only through `ci/iris.sh` and `ci/warm.sh`, with lane C's three variables;
  - `ocutalon-slot-c`, namespace `OCUTALON`, only for `iris_doc_load` compiles, and only after `iris_server_info` shows build 154 on port 52792.
